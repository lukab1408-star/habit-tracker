# Habit Tracker

A small offline habit tracker you can add to your phone's home screen.

**Open it:** https://lukab1408-star.github.io/habit-tracker/

- iPhone: open the link in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**
- Android: open the link in Chrome → ⋮ → **Install app**

Features: unlimited habits, several completions per day, daily/weekly/monthly goals, streaks and skip days, year-long tile grids, a calendar to edit past days, notes, charts and statistics, categories, icons and colors, archive and restore, backup export/import, and import from a HabitKit export file.

Your data stays on your device (browser storage). Use **Export backup** now and then to keep a copy.
